🧠 Hydration & ADHD Brain Function

⚡ Executive Stamina The prefrontal cortex requires optimal hydration for working memory. Just 1.5% dehydration triggers sluggish task-switching and cognitive fatigue.
💊 Medication Balance Stimulant ADHD medications increase fluid turnover and dry mouth. Consistent hydration prevents afternoon medication crashes and headaches.
👀 Visual Priming Rule Keep a filled, transparent water bottle directly in your visual periphery. ADHD brains rely on physical visual prompts rather than internal thirst cues.

Help & Documentation

Guiding principles on how to get the most out of your ADHD Life Planner Pro system.

How Your Data is Saved

Everything you write, check, or log is saved locally and instantly in your browser's persistent storage. No account is required, no external server can read your thoughts, and the application works 100% offline.

The "I'm Overwhelmed" Protocol

When cognitive load strikes, click the "I'm Overwhelmed" button. It activates an emergency calming screen with a guided 4-7-8 breathing circle, pauses notification noise, and prompts you for just one single micro-step.

🌿 Overwhelm Reset Protocol

Take a deep breath. You are safe, and you do not have to do everything right now. Let's do a 60-second reset.

Inhale 4s

6-Step Reset Action

  1. Pause. Stop trying to juggle 5 thoughts at once.
  2. Drink water. Sip half a glass slowly.
  3. Breathe. Complete 3 rounds of 4-7-8 breathing above.
  4. Brain dump. Get everything onto paper or into the Brain Dump tab.
  5. Choose 1 tiny action. Something that takes less than 3 minutes.
  6. Start a 5-minute timer. Just work for 5 minutes, then pause.
